Free Online URL Shortener & Custom Link Generator

Shortify is a free, fast, and reliable URL shortener built for creators, marketers, students, and businesses. Turn messy, long web addresses into clean, memorable short links in seconds — complete with custom aliases, optional link expiration, real-time click tracking, and instant downloadable QR codes.

How to Shorten a Link in 3 Simple Steps

1

Paste Your Long URL

Copy any long link from YouTube, Instagram, Google Drive, GitHub, or your website and paste it into the Shortify link box above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Shortify URL Shortener completely free to use?

Yes, Shortify is 100% free. You can shorten unlimited links, create custom URL aliases, generate QR codes, and view click statistics with no sign-up or hidden limits.

How do I create a QR code for my shortened link?

As soon as you click Shorten URL, Shortify automatically generates a high-resolution PNG QR code for your short link. Simply click the Download QR button to save and share it on posters, flyers, or WhatsApp.

Can I set an expiration date on my short URLs?

Yes! Click Advanced Options before shortening your link and enter the number of days (between 1 and 365). After that period, the short link will automatically expire.

Does Shortify track how many clicks my link gets?

Yes, every short link created on Shortify records real-time click counts so you can monitor traffic and audience engagement effortlessly.
